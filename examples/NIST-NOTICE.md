# NIST test data

The CTC, FTC and D2MI cases in this folder (`drawing.pdf` + `model.stp`) are
unmodified files published by the National Institute of Standards and
Technology:

- CTC-01 … CTC-05, FTC-06 … FTC-11: NIST MBE PMI Validation and Conformance Testing
  <https://www.nist.gov/ctl/smart-connected-systems-division/smart-connected-manufacturing-systems-group/mbe-pmi-0>
- D2MI-904 … D2MI-908: NIST Design-to-Manufacturing Information (D2MI)
  <https://www.nist.gov/ctl/smart-connected-systems-division/smart-connected-manufacturing-systems-group/enabling-digital-0>

These are works of the US Government and are not subject to copyright
protection in the United States (17 U.S.C. § 105); NIST notes that they may be
subject to foreign copyright. They are included as test fixtures only and are
not part of the DrawMind3D product or its licence.

The SYN-* cases are synthetic and generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic.py`.
